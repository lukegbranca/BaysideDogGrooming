About Us

A Salon Built on Love & Care

At Bayside Dog Grooming Salon, we treat every dog like our own. Our experienced team is passionate about making your pet look and feel their absolute best — in a warm, calm, and friendly environment.

Dogs are able to have a bit of a play with each other while waiting their turn, keeping them relaxed and happy throughout their visit. We take pride in our work and look forward to welcoming you and your furry family member.

What We Offer

Our Grooming Services

Whether your pup needs a quick freshen-up or a full pamper session, we have a service to suit every dog and budget.

Full Groom

Bath, blow-dry, brush-out, breed-specific cut, ear clean and nail trim. Your dog leaves looking their absolute best.

Bath & Brush

A thorough shampoo and conditioning wash followed by a professional blow-dry and brush-out to keep coats healthy and shiny.

Nail Trim & File

Careful clipping and filing to keep your dog comfortable on their paws. Perfectly finished nails every time.

Puppy's First Groom

A gentle introduction to the salon experience — designed to build confidence and make grooming a positive routine from day one.

De-shedding Treatment

Specialised treatment to reduce shedding, perfect for double-coated breeds like retrievers and huskies.

Tidy-Up Trim

A neat scissor tidy of face, paws and sanitary areas to keep your dog looking fresh between full grooming appointments.
