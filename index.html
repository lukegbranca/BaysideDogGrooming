What We Offer

Tailored Grooming for Every Dog

From a quick freshen-up to a full pamper session — we offer a range of grooming services to suit every breed and every budget.

Full Groom

Bath, blow-dry, brush-out, breed-specific cut, ear clean and nail trim. Your dog leaves looking their absolute best.

Bath & Brush

Thorough shampoo and conditioning wash, professional blow-dry and brush-out to keep coats healthy and shiny.

Nail Trim & File

Careful clipping and filing to keep your dog comfortable on their paws. Perfectly finished nails every time.

Puppy's First Groom

A gentle introduction to the salon experience — designed to build confidence and make grooming a positive routine from day one.

De-shedding Treatment

Specialised treatment to reduce shedding — perfect for double-coated breeds like golden retrievers and huskies.

Tidy-Up Trim

A neat scissor tidy of face, paws and sanitary areas to keep your dog looking fresh between full grooming appointments.

Our Story

A Salon Built on Love & Care

At Bayside Dog Grooming Salon we treat every dog like our own. Our experienced team, led by Vanessa and her dedicated staff, is passionate about making your pet look and feel their absolute best — in a warm, calm and welcoming environment.

Dogs are able to have a bit of a play with each other while waiting their turns, keeping them relaxed and happy. From golden retrievers and schnauzers to pugs and cavoodles — every breed is welcome and every dog is given the care and attention they deserve.
